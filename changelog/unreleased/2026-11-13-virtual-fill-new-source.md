- **Virtual lists: new rows fill on their own budget.** When the index-built lists asking for rows change (another list in the old one's place, or the same list with any change to its keys, even one that keeps a header row) while the old fill was still waiting or had given up, the new rows start their own fill at once. They used to inherit the old fill's wait and its spent resumes, so they could stay blank until a scroll. The retired wait takes its timer with it, so it no longer wakes the host. Lists that are already filled, mounting or unmounting nearby, leave a fill alone.
